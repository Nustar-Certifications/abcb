About us

AB-CB is the leading accreditation body, known for its worldwide recognition, impartiality & Continuity. Our acceptance is Global for accreditation services, which recognise for best practices in quality assessment all over world. Being as an experienced accreditation body, AB-CB has accredited so many CAB’s and registered so many organisations & products. All CAB’s, organisations and products can be searched with verification of the authenticity by any AB-CB accredited certificate holder.

Our mission is to help CAB’s become more competitive worldwide by ensuring the competency as well as the continually improvement of organization’s management system.
