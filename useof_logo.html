Use of logo

Logo of AB-CB can be used after the permission of accreditation board. Detailed Information about using of AB-CB logos can be get from the accredited CAB.

Logo of AB-CB is only available to accredited CAB. Use of logo allows prospective users of accredited certification services to gain significant competitive advantage over their competitors. AB-CB logo is used by accredited CAB’s to show their services in the area of conformity assessment.

CAB’s are supposed to use logo on their websites, publicity material and Certificates. Use of AB-CB logo or its likeliness as company for any of the commercial use logo is prohibited. Certified organisation can use AB-CB Logo for activity in certification's scope. Certified organisation will have to stop use of AB-CB logo if expiring, cancelling or withdrawing of certificate or certification contract.
